Our services

Care for the whole person — and the whole family.

From psychiatric care to everyday support, we connect you with the services that help recovery actually stick. Not sure where to start? Call us and we'll talk it through.

Medication Management

Psychiatric evaluations, medication monitoring, refills, and ongoing support from a licensed provider.

Mental Health Evaluation

Comprehensive behavioral health assessments to understand what's going on and build the right plan with you.

Counseling & Group Therapy

Supportive individual and group therapy focused on emotional wellness, connection, and lasting change.

Life Skills Training

Practical coaching that helps you build independence and confidence in everyday living.

Housing Assistance

Help finding and keeping stable housing, with connections to local resources and benefits.

Transportation Assistance

Rides to medical appointments and free monthly bus passes so getting to care is never the barrier.

GED & Job Placement Support

Educational and workforce readiness services to help you reach the next step in your goals.

Parenting & Anger Management

Classes that support family wellness, emotional regulation, and healthier relationships at home.

Mentoring Program for Minors

Caring guidance and youth support that helps young people grow, connect, and thrive.

About us

Behavioral health, delivered with dignity.

Peaceful Behavioral Services is a Maryland behavioral health provider supporting adults, youth, and families with psychiatric care and the community services that make recovery possible. We meet people where they are — without judgment, and without making you jump through hoops to get help.

  • Compassion First. We treat every individual with dignity, empathy, and respect.
  • Accessible & Responsive Care. We provide timely, responsive care when it is needed most.
  • Whole-Person Support. We support mental wellness, daily living, housing resources, and transportation.
